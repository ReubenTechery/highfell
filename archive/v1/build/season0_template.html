Making your own

Five taps, about a minute

A new player builds a hero from the same kit as the six. Everything past these five is earned.

  1. 1RacePick a body. Your hometown and its light come with it.
  2. 2FaceSkin and hair from that race's range. None of it is locked or paid.
  3. 3ClassSix jobs. Class colours and starter gear go on at once.
  4. 4NameA first name and a family name. Brannoc Ashvale, not xXSlayerXx.
  5. 5LightYour hometown's backdrop. More are earned in raids, never bought.
