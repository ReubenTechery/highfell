    Barrow Crypt

    Three seats: tank, healer and DPS. Pick yours and two bots fill the others. In the real game, those seats are your mates. Five packs, a mini boss, a three-phase boss, then everyone rolls on the chest.

      Controls
      Phone
      • Drag your left thumb to move.
      • Swipe anywhere to dodge-roll.
      • Buttons on the right. Hold the tank's shield. Drag Blizzard or Prayer circle to aim.
      • Healer: tap a name to heal.
      • Swipe through wisps and chains. Tap cages.
      Keyboard
      • WASD or arrows to move, Space to dodge.
      • 1, 2, 3 for abilities. Hold 2 to block as the tank.
      • Healer: Z, X, C heal the tank, you, the DPS.
      • Click and drag through wisps and chains, or E to cut. Esc pauses.

      The Gravekeeper is buried

      The chest has three things in it. Need if it's for your role, greed if you want it anyway. Highest roll wins.
