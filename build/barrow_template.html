The first room

The Barrow

One room. Eight stations. Six classes, three jobs each.

It isn't just a set. A set is something you look at. The Barrow is a machine you play: eight stations you touch, open, light and fight at, each one built to take a thousand parties' worth of hands. Nobody swings anything.

From above

Six metres square, in and out through the inn

One door from the Last Lantern into the Barrow, and the same door home. The GM sits in a nook beside the room with a camera on every station, and the bar is the check-in.

The chamber

The stations

Eight things you can put your hands on

Every station is something real to touch, open, light or slide. The delicate magic (light, sound, haze and projection) sits out of reach, above and behind.

%%STATIONS%%
Class jobs

Six tools, three jobs each, in this room alone

Every class carries one tool and gets at least three moments where only they can act. Each tool has a tag inside it: touch it to the right rune, lock or plate and the room responds.

%%CLASSES%%

Fewer than six heroes? Anyone can pick up a second tool. And every class job is a shortcut, not a lock: a party with no rogue can still find the reliquary key. It just costs them time.

The run

Four acts, one hour, and the inn either side

Every visit runs 90 minutes door to door. The hour in the Barrow has four acts, and each act hands the spotlight to different classes.

    %%ACTS%%

When the King falls, the sealed door swings open on the inn. The party rolls for loot at the bar, the fastest clears go up on the board, and the innkeeper books the next chapter.

The loot roll

Why you'd raid it again

The puzzles get you through the door. The loot brings you back. Every hero who clears the Barrow rolls two d10s at the bar, read as 1 to 100.

  • CommonBarrow dust or a bone charm for your hero page1–70
  • RareThe Ghostlight Lantern, a glowing lantern for your hero71–94
  • EpicThe title Gravewarden, shown under your name95–99
  • LegendaryThe Bone Crown, plus a real enamel pin handed over the bar100

Rules of the roll

  • One roll per hero per week. Raid as often as you like; the loot resets every Monday, like a raid lockout.
  • Heroic mode opens after your first clear: harder rolls, and the crown drops on 98 to 100.
  • Fastest clear of the week goes on the board above the hearth.
  • Loot comes with the ticket. It can't be bought, sold or traded, so it stays a game, not a gamble.

Different every run

  • Three versions of every riddle. The engine deals one per run.
  • The three keys in act II can come in any order.
  • The King fights one of three ways, and you won't know which until he rises.
  • Each new chapter changes who lies in the Barrow and what they want. The stations stay.
Built for a thousand hands

Built like an arcade, not a film set

At full stretch the Barrow sees about a thousand parties a year: five thousand people pulling, pressing and prodding everything in it. Film sets are built to last a shoot. This is built to last years.
