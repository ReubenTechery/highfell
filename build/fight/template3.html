The Barrow King

You're Brannoc Ashvale, the dwarf warrior. Four bots fill out your party. Put the king back to sleep before his song ends.

  • %%T_chop%%Chop builds rage. Overhead spends it on a big hit. Your hits also keep his eyes on you.
  • %%T_taunt%%Taunt when he shoves you and turns on someone else. The screen goes red when you've lost him.
  • %%T_shout%%Shout puts a shield on everyone. Do it just before the pulse, when his big hits land.
  • %%T_shatter%%Shatter on Ivy's “Now!” Every 20 seconds she freezes him. Perfect timing is triple damage.
  • %%T_ult%%Big Swing, once a fight. Twice as hard while he's frozen.

A glowing button means press it now. Sound is on. On a keyboard: 1 to 6, space for Shatter.
