Raid I · The Singing Barrow

The Barrow King

You're Brannoc Ashvale, the dwarf warrior. Four bots fill out your party. Put the king back to sleep before his song ends.

  • Chop and Overhead. Smash them whenever they light up. Auto attack runs by itself.
  • Taunt. When he turns on someone else, pull him back. Only you can.
  • Shout. Shield the party just before the pulse. His big hits land on it.
  • Shatter. Every 20 seconds Ivy freezes him. Hit it on her “Now!”
  • Big Swing. Once a fight. Twice as hard while he's frozen.

Sound is on. On a keyboard, use 1 to 6, and space for Shatter.
