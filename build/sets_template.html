Season 1 · The Long Frost

Season 1 sets

One set per class, cut from the season's story. Raids drop the pieces. The power resets when the season ends, and the look is yours for good.

Rimeguard is built in the kit. The other five are sketches, waiting their turn.

Set rules

Looks first, power second

4 pieces

Helm, shoulders, body, weapon

Each piece swaps one layer of the kit, so a half-finished set still looks deliberate.

2 and 4

Bonuses help your job

Two pieces give a small boost to what only your class can do. Four give a bigger one. Never raw damage.

Heroic

Harder, not stronger

Heroic drops the same set in new colours, with a title and the full-set aura. Same power.

Forever

Power resets, looks stay

When the season ends the bonuses switch off. Every set you earned stays in your wardrobe.

The six sets

One built, five in the dark

A shadow means the set is named, coloured and joked, but not drawn yet. Pick the next one and it gets built into the kit the same way Rimeguard was. Bonuses are first guesses for the paper playtest.
