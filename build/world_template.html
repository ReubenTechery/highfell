Lore voice

Tell it like the pub would

Four rules for every line of lore, whoever writes it. They're also what Claude gets checked against.

1

A story, not a textbook

Short sentences and one twist. No dates, no family trees, no ancient wars with numbers in them.

2

Two sentences a card

If it needs more, it's two cards. People read lore between turns, not instead of them.

3

One joke, one mystery

Every place gets something funny and something nobody can explain yet. The mystery is next season.

4

No chosen ones

The heroes are whoever turned up on the night. Nobody is born special. They just didn't go home.

Too much

“In the year 1042 of the Long Reckoning, Varnoth the Pale, last heir of the Ice Courts, swore his oath in the Hall of Nine Winters, and the Vale knew sorrow.”

Ours

“The Frost King has been asleep a long time. Somebody just woke him up.”
