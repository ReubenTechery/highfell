# Highfell Dungeon Systems

> A copy of the Dungeon Systems doc on claude.ai, made 14 Oct 2026, so every agent can read it. Keep it
> true: when a system changes, change it here in the same pull request. Where it and the code disagree about
> what's built, the code wins; for what's planned, this doc wins.

Dungeons and their collection logs are the core loop of the whole game: OSRS endgame PvE style grind-loot-RNG runs (~3 to 4 minutes in starter gear, ~2 minutes when melted in full BiS) with four buttons and a swipe, where you grind a dungeon 100+ times to fill its collection log, feel steep power creep as your Warband gears up, unlock Heroic mode, and move on to the next dungeon (where future dungeons also drop OSRS-style upgrade attachments that awaken older dungeon gear). Raids are a later bonus activity, not the core loop.

## Core Loop & Dungeon Rules

1. **Dungeons + Collection Logs are the core loop.** A run takes about 3 to 4 minutes at the door (Barrow Crypt about 3½ in Common gear at level 1; a later dungeon at the level and Power it recommends), melts to about 1:15 with the dungeon's set at the level you'd have it, and goes under a minute as your level climbs (18 Oct). It needs all three roles and drops loot every time.
2. **Four buttons plus the swipe dodge, never more.** Sets, weapon traits, and boss weapons transform what buttons do; they never add one.
3. **Dramatic power creep, long collection chase.** Regular gear climbs Common → Rare in 8 clears (a short step, to `1.24×`), while Epic 3-piece sets (1/25 per piece) and Boss Weapons (1/40) push power up to `2.06×` (plus crit and summons), the same in every dungeon of the season. After that the climb is your level: every level makes the whole party 2.5% stronger, to 3.45× at 99 and on past it, so grinding 100+ runs feels noticeably faster and more explosive. Talents (a point a level to 30, four picks of two) decide how you play, and a full tree clears about a fifth faster.
4. **Normal solo with your Warband, Heroic in a group.** Normal dungeons can be run with your 3-hero Warband (so gearing your alts directly speeds up your runs; they come to help and never take loot) or with a group if you want, and a Normal collection log can always be finished solo. Heroic is the optional group flex: it drops the Normal loot, so grouping up is never a worse evening than playing solo, plus gilding kits (gilded looks at the same power) in a log of their own. It needs at least two real players; with two, a bot takes the third seat, and your Warband heroes never sit in. There is no online play yet, so Heroic stays locked (the "Everything open" testing switch lets you in).
5. **No drinking for mana and no waiting around:** mana refills between pulls.
6. **A run never gets thrown away by a slip:** on a phone the back swipe (or back button) pauses the run instead of leaving the game, and closing or reloading the tab asks first where the browser allows it (inside claude.ai's frame only the ask, since its history isn't ours). Added 17 Oct after a play tester lost two runs mid-boss. As built: `src/ui/runguard.ts`, armed by `start()` and let go by `stop()` in `run.ts`.

## Combat, Difficulty & Boss Mechanics

**The beat and the press queue** (`docs/combat.md`; `src/dungeon/press.js`):
- The four buttons and Mend share a **1.0 s beat**: firing one starts it, and the others on it wait until it ends (a thin sweep runs round them, as WoW's does). Charge, Shadowstep, the shield and the dodge never wait for it or start it. While the tank's shield is up, buttons on the beat wait too, so a held Cleave can't keep reopening the perfect-block window. Bots wait for it as well (`ready()`).
- **A press is never thrown away:** one made up to 0.4 s before its button can fire waits (a small bright ring round the button) and fires the moment it can; one made during a dodge, a Charge or a cast waits for it to end and then for the beat, so Charge into Cleave is one move. A tap to Mend waits the whole beat. One press waits at a time: the newest replaces it, a dodge clears it, and it lapses after about a beat. A press too early to keep is let go with a pulse of the button's ring, and nothing buzzes. The buzz is only for a real "can't" (no mana, nothing in reach).
- **Holding:** on the phone a held button presses again whenever it can (not the shield, held to block, nor the two you drag to aim), and its repeats stay quiet. On a computer each key press counts, as in WoW: a held key fires once, except the shield's, which blocks for as long as it's held.
- **The buttons on a phone:** the filler is the big button in the bottom corner, under the thumb; the payoff (the tank's shield) sits above it, and the two cooldowns and the roll further out. On a computer they're a row, E, Q, F, R and Space, the filler biggest. Every button shrinks a little the moment your finger or key goes down.
- **Keys** (`src/content/keys.ts`, yours in `src/ui/keybinds.ts`): each class's buttons on E, Q, F and R, the filler on E. Tank: Cleave E, shield Q, Charge F, Slam R. Healer: Smite E, Ward Q, Prayer circle F, Sunburst R. Mage: Icicle E, Comet Q, Blizzard F, Rime burst R. Rogue: Viper Blades E, Shadowstrike Q, Fan of Blades F, Shadowstep R. 1 to 4 still work, WASD or the arrows move, Space (or Shift) dodges, and Z, X and C Mend the tank, you and the DPS. **Settings' Keys list** (on a computer) rebinds your class's buttons and the dodge to a key, Shift and a key, or a mouse button (not the left); W, A, S, D, the arrows, Esc, P and the healer's Z, X and C are kept, and a key another button has swaps the two. They're kept in this browser (`highfell.keys`), never in the save. If a button uses Shift, Shift on its own stops dodging. A browser shortcut (Ctrl, Cmd or Alt with a key) never fires a button. The buttons, the coach and the Crypt Trial's lessons show your keys.

**Each class's rotation** (`src/dungeon/buttons.js`; numbers before Power):
- **Tank:** Cleave every beat (60, a wide arc, 2× threat). It **Crushes** (1.6×, 3× threat) after a Charge (two with the Gravedigger), a Slam, a perfect block (which also ends the beat) or a Cleave crit, and always on a stunned, Staggered or Exposed foe. An automatic swing for 25 every 1.5 s.
- **Healer:** Mend (tap a name) is 230 for 9 mana on the beat; Smite every quiet beat (60 to the tank's target, half of it healing your most hurt mate, +4 mana, pulses your Prayer circles; a red bomb in reach first). A Smite crit **blesses** the next Mend: free and half again as big, and the frames glow gold. Heals crit for 1.5× on the same chance as hits.
- **Mage:** Icicle every beat (62; frozen foes Shatter for 3×, a Chilled boss 1.5×). An Icicle crit makes the next Icicle **Shatter** anything. Comet, Blizzard and Rime burst (10 s, freezes for 4 s, stops casts) as before. No automatic attack: the mage moves freely, and only Comet's cast stands still.
- **Rogue:** Viper Blades every beat (38, venom, +1 point, +2 on a crit); Fan of Blades +1 point if it hits, Shadowstep +2. **Shadowstrike** spends every point, 24 a point, 1.35× and always a crit on a poisoned foe. An automatic swing for 16 every 0.7 s.
- **Exposed** is every payoff's setup: Cleave Crushes, Icicle and Comet Shatter as on a Chilled boss, Shadowstrike counts as five points (once a window), and Smite heals double.
- **Your hits feel it** (`FEEL` and `hold()` in `entities.js`, drawn by `render.js` and `drawfx.js`): a filler's hit holds you and the target still on screen for a moment (a crit and a payoff longer) while the fight goes on; a crit's number reads "N!", tilts and bounces; a payoff's is gold with its word; numbers step up rather than pile on one another. Small mobs squash and are nudged back; a boss flashes on your filler, crits and payoffs; kills fling small mobs and collapse big ones; an Exposed mob wears a gold edge. Only your own hits shake or freeze the screen, and only crits, payoffs, big kills and perfect blocks shake it. The gold glow is for payoffs only (CRUSH!, SHATTER, FINISH!); warnings (BLOCK!, KICK!, TAUNT!, PEEL!, SHIELD!, CLEANSE!, DODGE!) keep their words without it.

**Threat & Crushing Blows:** holding mobs is the tank's job, not a given. The tank's hits make a third more threat than anyone else's, and Cleave twice that (a Crush three times), so a tank pressing Cleave every beat holds a pack against a mage pressing Icicle every beat; heals pull a little on everything in the fight. A mob switches once someone beats its target by a tenth. A mob on anyone but the tank shows a red "!!" straight away, and when something turns on you (and you're not the tank) an orange ring under you says get to the tank. Bot DPS hold a big hit that would pull a boss off the tank (`pullsBoss` in `bots.js`).

- **Slam** taunts everything around you for 4 seconds and drags it in to arm's reach (big mobs half as far, bosses not at all). Its hits make double threat.
- **Charge** makes the mob you hit turn on you; Ringing Blow makes everything it hits turn.
- A mob that isn't on the tank for 3 seconds goes **wild**: it hits harder and faster.
- **Crushing Blows (`1.85×`):** Bosses and Brutes (`m.isBoss || m.M.big`) landing a melee hit on a non-tank (`role !== 'tank'`) deal **1.85× Crushing Blow** damage with an alarm callout (`"Crushing blow! Let the tank hold him!"`), so a Rogue or Mage cannot facetank bosses.
- **Casts and interrupts:** a cast with the green bar stops only to an interrupt (Charge, Rime burst, Fan of Blades, Shadowstep), a stun, a freeze or a perfect block, never to damage, and the caster is Exposed for 3 s ("Interrupted!"). Gravecallers cast **Grave Bolt** (200 at a random hero, a 2 s cast every 9 s) and **Raise Dead**. **Menders** (the Barrow's Gravemender in pack D, Thimblebriar's Gnome Medic in the Root Tunnel) cast a 2 s heal of 30% on their most hurt packmate every 6 s: kill them first, or kick them. Bot DPS go for a cast to kick, then a mender, then Gravecallers. A bot party kicks about 5 times a Barrow run.
- **Pulls:** each pack is fodder plus one or two jobs. The Barrow: A 4 Rattlers and a Gravecaller; B the Bonebrute and 2 Rattlers; C 2 Archers, a Rattler and a Gravecaller; D a Gravecaller, the Gravemender and 2 Rattlers; E the ambush, 4 Rattlers and the Bonebrute. Common bots take 13 to 18 s a pack, and the packs are 42% of the fighting.
- In the Gravekeeper's last phase **only the tank is caged**. Inside, nothing can target him and every mob forgets half his threat, so the boss turns on someone else until he mashes out (or a mate breaks him out) and Slams it back. Breaking a cage in under `2.0s` exposes the boss and resets the Tank's `Charge` cooldown.
- **Curses (Healer role mechanic, every room but the Gravekeeper's):** Gravecallers (packs A, C and D, and one that rises with Sir Rattlebones' rally) curse a hero 1.8s into their fight and every 8.5s after: a purple skull with a 4-second ring over their head, their name purple in the frames. Only the **Healer** lifts it, by swiping across them (from anywhere on the screen, the walk stick's half too) or with `Sunburst`, for +5 mana. Left alone, it bursts and drops its hero to 10% of their health; on its own it never kills, so every miss is a chance for a save. Curses end with the fight or the hero, and never come in the Crypt Trial. Bot DPS kill Gravecallers first (after kickable casts), so a bot party sees about four curses a run. A curse can't be missed: it lands with a low toll and a falling wail (louder for the healer and the cursed hero), and the cursed mate's party frame (the heal pad, bottom right, and the frames at the top) turns purple with the skull, its ring and the seconds left. Until you've lifted three curses (`curse.lifts` in `p.counters`, `needsSwipeHint`), a gold arrow sweeps across the cursed mate and a dark one across their frame. A swipe across a cursed mate's frame lifts it too (`bindFrameSwipes` in `src/ui/input.ts`); a tap on a frame still only heals.
- **Saves:** the healer's heal landing on a mate under 15% pops a gold `SAVED!` with a chime and a short stop. As the healer, a mate under 15% throbs red in the frames and a heartbeat plays.
- **Chains and Wisps (Healer role mechanic):** only the **Healer** can swipe across chains to sever them (exposing the boss for 3s) or swipe/smite Wisps to banish them (triggering a `Soul Spark` backlash bolt on the boss and restoring +5 mana).
- **Numbers:** you see only your own (your hits, your heals, what hits you). Your mates' spell effects still show.

**Difficulty & Avoidable Telegraphs:** Barrow Crypt's `tune` scales all hero damage taken by `1.5×` and trash HP by `1.65×` (`hp: { brute: 1.4, keeper: 0.55, knight: 0.93 }`; `heroic`: `hit: 3.4, trashHp: 2.35, bossHp: 1.65`). Each dungeon's `tune` lives in its engine file: `hit` scales everything that hurts a hero, `trashHp` the health of everything but the bosses, `bossHp` the bosses', `hp` gives a named mob its own health multiplier instead, and `dmg` makes a named mob's ordinary swings hit harder (never its moves or bombs; Thimblebriar's Tinker King). The Gravekeeper's two-phase Normal fight has 55% of his written health; Heroic gives him all of it, times `bossHp`. Ground hazards and boss telegraphs (`hazard: true`) bypass passive armor unless actively blocked AND **pierce 60% of `Ward` shields**, so standing in `Shield Charge`, `Cleaving Sweep`, `Burrow Erupt`, `Grasping Hands`, or `Shockwave Ring` in beginner gear will nearly kill you even through a shield.

**Boss `Exposed` Windows (`+35%` damage taken):**
- Perfect-blocking a heavy boss attack stuns the boss, primes `Crush`, and exposes the boss for `3.5s`.
- Interrupting a boss's cast (Sir Rattlebones' `Bone rattle`, the Gravekeeper's `Grave cold` and Heroic `Grave dust`, Old Toadstool's `Spore Burst`: 2 s casts with the green bar) exposes him for `3s`; missed, it lands on the whole party as before. A boss's window calls `EXPOSED! Hit him now!` for everyone.
- Dodging Sir Rattlebones's `Shield Charge` so he misses every hero makes him crash into the tomb wall (`Wall Crash! Exposed!` for `3.0s`).
- Killing Sir Rattlebones's Phase 2 rallied guards lashes him for 4% max HP each, and killing the last guard cracks his rally armour and exposes him for `3.0s`.
- Cutting the Gravekeeper's `Soul Chain` exposes him for `3.0s`, and breaking `Bone Cage` in `< 2.0s` exposes him for `2.5s` while resetting the Tank's `Charge`.

**Combos on bosses** (`chillBoss`, `staggerBoss`, `stunned` and `shatterable` in `src/dungeon/entities.js`): a boss never freezes, never gets stunned by a knock and never gets dragged, but the combos still land on him.
- `Rime burst` leaves a boss **Chilled** for `4s` (a light frost tint and a `Chilled` callout) and slows him a little. `Icicle` and `Comet` shatter a Chilled (or Exposed) boss for `1.5×` (frozen trash takes `3×` and `2×`), with the same `Shatter!` text, flash and sound. With **Shatterlance**, Comet hits a Chilled boss `2×`; **Rimebone 2pc** gives back half of Comet's cooldown on a Chilled boss too; the **Gravefrost Staff**'s Comet Chills a boss for `2s`.
- `Charge` and the **Gravedigger**'s tremor leave a boss **Staggered** for `1s` (stars over his head and a `Staggered` callout). It is not a stun: he keeps swinging and his telegraphs stay. It counts as a stun for `Cleave`'s Crush and for **Headsman** (every hit crits).
- `Slam` taunts a boss and spins its whirlwind through him, with no drag. A perfect block still stuns a boss for `1.6s`. Blizzard and Icicles hit a boss but never slow him.
- A bot mage steps into Rime burst's reach at a calm moment (the boss is on the tank, between moves, and nobody is caged), Chills him, then steps back out (`chillTarget` in `src/dungeon/bots.js`).

**The Gravekeeper on Normal: two phases, about 65 seconds** (`keeperTick` in `src/content/barrow/bosses.js`; 55% of his written health):

## XP, Levels & Talents

Built 17 Oct, at the owner's call after a play tester said a run often gave nothing and the gear chase didn't feel like it changed anything. Every kill pays XP you can see, your level gives talent points, and each hero spends them in one of its class's two spec trees. On 18 Oct (the owner's "two hats" pass, `docs/ideas.md`) levels became the climb: every Epic in a season is the same Power, and every level makes your whole party stronger, to 99 and on past it for ever. It replaced "no hero levels or skill trees" (pillar 2 in `AGENTS.md`), then "gear carries most of the power".

**XP and your level**
- **One level for all your heroes** (`p.xp`, like champion points). Level 2 is 1,000 XP, and each level asks a little more: `1,000 + 350·(L−1) + 10·(L−1)²` (`levelNeed` in `src/content/xp.ts`, added up in one go by `xpForLevel`). Level 30, a full tree, is about 248,000 XP; 50 about 841,000; 99, the top of the long climb, about 4.85 million. **There is no top:** past 99 the levels keep coming on the same curve, about 30 runs of the later dungeons each.
- **Every level makes your whole party stronger** (`levelBonus` in `src/content/xp.ts`): +2.5% to every hit, heal, shield and health of every seat (you, your Warband and the bots, who stand in at your level) a level from 2 up to 99, and +0.5% a level after it. Level 22 is +52.5%, 30 is +72.5%, 99 is +245% (3.45×), 100 +245.5%. The run passes it to the engine (`might`, `partyBonus` in `src/meta/xp.ts`), which multiplies every seat's power and health by it; crit chance still comes from the gear alone. A level that comes in the middle of a fight grows the party at once (`strengthen` in `src/dungeon/entities.js`), keeping the health they'd lost. The Talents tab says what yours is ("+55%: your level makes your whole party stronger"), the level burst in a run says what the new one added ("+2.5% to your party"), and so does the chest ("Your party +57.5%").
- **Every kill pays the moment it dies**, popped in violet over the mob: a wisp 5, a skeleton 25, an archer 30, a caller or mender 35, a brute 90, a mini boss 400, the last boss 800 (`KILL_XP`); bombs and other things nothing. It goes into your save as it comes and is written out within three seconds (at once on a new level, when you leave the run or when the page hides), so a wipe, leaving or a phone closing the tab keeps it. A wisp that reaches you and bursts pays nothing.
- **Every chest pays more:** 400 for the clear, 1 to 10 Lore Pages at 100 each (1 at 50%, 2 at 25%, 3 at 15%, 5 at 8%, 10 at 2%), and two tomes, each rolled on its own like any drop: a Scholar's Tome at 1/25 (+2,500) and a Grand Tome at 1/500 (+25,000, with its own moment and a Share card). They show as cards in the chest; neither is a collection log slot.
- **Harder pays more:** Thimblebriar (tier 2) pays 1.5×, a tier-3 dungeon 2×, and Heroic 1.5× on top (`xpMul` in `src/meta/xp.ts`). A Barrow clear in Common gear pays about 3,000 XP (about 2,150 from kills), so a full tree takes about 80 Barrow clears, fewer in harder dungeons.
- **In a run** your own party frame shows your level and a thin violet bar; a new level bursts in the middle ("Level 12!", "+1 talent point · +2.5% to your party"). **The chest's numbers** show the run's XP (kills, clear, pages, tomes), your level's bar, a level gained with its points and your party's bonus, and a Talents button when there are points to spend. A run that doesn't count (the Crypt Trial, test gear) pays none.
- **Levels 30, 50 and 99** are achievements (Full Tree; Seasoned with the title "the Seasoned"; Ninety-Nine with "the Tireless"), on the achievements page under Your level (`src/content/levels.ts`).
- **Old saves** were paid once for what they had done (1,500 a clear and each boss kill's XP, at today's multipliers), and a toast says so the first time they load.
- **Seasons:** when a new season comes, levels reset with the gear (the owner's call, 18 Oct; `docs/ideas.md`). Nothing about seasons is built yet.

**What each dungeon asks:** the first dungeon of a season is made for Common at level 1; each later one recommends a level and a Power (`recLevel`, `recIlvl` on its `DungeonInfo`) and is tuned so the bots at that level, in plain gear of that Power, clear it in about 3½ minutes. Thimblebriar recommends level 15 and Power 22 (its tune is its old one times level 15's bonus: `hit: 4.05, trashHp: 6.35, bossHp: 1.62`, the Stomper 4.86, the Tinker King 1.24); Moonmere, still to come, level 30 and Power 26. Heroic Barrow is made for level 20 in full Epic and Heroic Thimblebriar for level 35 in its own kit. The Dungeons sheet says "Recommended level 15, Power 22 · Orvik level 8, Power 14", and the warning weighs the two together: your strength is your gear's power times your level's bonus, so a higher level makes up for weaker gear and the other way round (`underPowered`, `strengthOf` in `src/meta/party.ts`). Short by a fifth or more of the strength it wants: "You’ll very likely be wiped."

**Talent points and specs**
- **One point a level from level 2**, up to 29 at level 30, the same points for every hero of yours (`pointsAt` in `src/meta/talents.ts`). Each hero picks one of its class's two specs, free to switch, and each spec keeps its own points, so switching back costs nothing.
- **A tree has five rows.** Row 1 is open; rows 2 to 5 open with 5, 10, 15 and 20 points in the rows above (`ROW_NEED`). Stat talents take 3 to 5 points, each point adding `+1%` damage or healing, `+2%` health, `+1%` crit chance, cooldowns `3%` faster (never the beat), `1.5%` less damage taken or `+6%` threat (`TALENT_STATS`). The rest take one point and change how a button works, never adding one. **Every row from 2 is a pick of two** (row 5's are the two capstones), so a full tree is four picks: sixteen builds a spec (18 Oct; before, only row 3 was a pick and a full tree held every talent but one). A full tree is 29 points. Points come back out free (unless a row below needs them), and so does a whole tree. Ids never changed, so every point a save had is where it was: a talent that became one of a pair is that pair's first.

## Heroes, Warband and Alts

A hero is one race and one class, picked when you make them and fixed after. You can have six, and the one you play stands by the fire on the Hearth.

- **The Warband opens later:** a new player has one hero. Until every dungeon has been cleared once (each one's Normal: the Barrow and Thimblebriar today), bots in plain gear at your hero's level take the other two seats, the bunk room's empty bed is locked (a padlock and "Warband"; tapping it says which dungeons are left), Your heroes and Who goes in say the same, and the welcome tour skips the bunk room (two steps, not three). The clear that opens it says so ("Your Warband is open"), and the next visit to the door shows the Your Warband tip. Once open it stays open (counter `warband.open`), so a dungeon added later never shuts it. Saves that already have more than one hero, and anyone who has earned a new race from a log, have it open. Why: you fall for one main first; alts come because you want more, not as homework. As built: `src/meta/warband.ts` (`warbandOpen`, `warbandLeft`, `openWarband`, `warbandWhy`), used by `createHero`, `seatsFor` and `doorTip`; `tests/warband.test.ts`. Built 17 Oct.

- **The bunk room & Warband party seats:** every hero you aren't playing waits by their bed in the house. Tap one to play them, check their gear, rename them (once) or let one go (the last one stays); an empty bed makes a new hero. When you enter a **Normal** dungeon, your own heroes from your bunk room (`seats(p, false)` in `src/meta/party.ts`) fill the other two role seats wearing their real equipped gear, sets, traits, unique weapons, and sprites, best-geared first, when their gear is at least as good as the bot's (`helps`: their Power against the plain gear bots wear for you, `partyRar`); otherwise a bot (Brannoc, Maren or Ivy) takes the seat, and Who goes in says which hero to gear up. They come to help and never get loot: every drop is yours (17 Oct, the owner's call: your one hero is the main character). Or you can group with friends. **Heroic** needs at least two real players (`partyProblem` in `src/meta/party.ts`); with two, a bot takes the third seat, and your Warband heroes never sit in (`seatsFor`). With no online play yet, Heroic stays locked, except with the "Everything open" testing switch.
- **Each hero keeps:** their regular gear (the Common to Rare ladder), their equipped uniques, their title and their clears.
- **What each slot wears is what you see:** a hero is drawn as a body (race, sex and class stance, in plain underclothes) with a layer for each gear slot (chest with its shield or cape, shoulders, helm, weapon), so a rag cap stays a rag cap under an iron chest and Barrowguard pauldrons. Each class's gear is drawn once, on one reference body (the warrior's is the dwarf, the rogue's the human), and fitted to every other body by its anchors (head, shoulder line, hips, shoulders and hands); only the shapes move, so the black line keeps its weight. A gilded piece turns gold on its own. Bots wear plain gear at your party's level. The run bakes its three heroes once, front and back, and lets them go when it ends. Built 17 Oct; it fixed putting one piece on drawing the whole set, and made the page about 1 MB lighter. ## The Garden & Brews

Instead of riding out to a separate map or waiting on real-time timers, the Hearth's outdoor yard holds **3 planter beds** (`bed.0`, `bed.1`, `bed.2`) that feed directly into the dungeon loop:

1. **Seeds from dungeon bosses:** Each dungeon drops its own seed from its final boss. In Barrow Crypt, The Gravekeeper drops **Glowcap** seeds (`1/5` on Normal, `1/3` on Heroic). Saves from before the garden were given one Glowcap seed to try it; a new player's first seed comes from the Gravekeeper (never on their first run). One-step tips show the way the first time (see Onboarding): the beds when the seed drops, the bed when it's ready, the cauldron when the herbs make a brew, and the brew button on the door with the first bottle.
2. **Grow in 3 dungeon clears (`GROW_CLEARS = 3`):** Tap an empty bed in the yard to plant a seed. Each Normal or Heroic dungeon clear advances all planted beds by 1 stage (`growBeds`; `0/3` sprout → `1/3`, `2/3` growing → `3/3` ready). The Crypt Trial does not advance beds, and crops never wilt.
3. **Harvest in close-up:** Tap a ready bed to harvest in close-up (`snip` a stem on the dotted line or `pluck` a glowcap at its brightest). Three cuts per harvest: base yield is `8–10` herbs, each Perfect cut adds `+1` (up to `+3`), and Gathering ranks 4, 7 and 10 each add `+1` more. **Every cut pays Gathering XP as it lands**, with a bar at the foot of the close-up and a burst on a new rank.
4. **Rares & Starlight Dew:** Your very first harvest grants the **Hill Goat** (`mount.goat`). Every harvest also rolls `GARDEN_TABLE` independently (OSRS style), has a `1/4` chance to shimmer and drop `1–2` **Starlight Dew**, and converts any duplicate rare into `2` Starlight Dew.
5. **Brew at the cauldron:** Stirring at the cauldron by your fire turns herbs into bottles (every lap pays Brewing XP; a Perfect stir fills 1 extra bottle). The ingredients go in when the stir starts, so leaving mid-stir still bottles it.

### Party-wide brews (one bottle per run)

Brews are strong **party-wide speed and power buffs** that make Normal, Heroic, and Raid runs noticeably faster (~20% faster clears) without ever being mandatory to clear.
- **One bottle poured at the door (`pourFor`):** Only **one** bottle is uncorked at the dungeon door for the whole 3-seat party (your hero's chosen brew first, then Tank → Healer → DPS). Even if multiple party members bring a brew selected, only the single poured bottle is spent (`spendBottle`), so teammates never waste duplicate potions.

## Gear and sets

The weapon is the upgrade you feel; Helm, Shoulders and Chest are the set. Regular gear is a short climb, `1.00` at Common (score 10) to `1.24` at Rare (score 14); the long chase is where gear's power is, `1.72` at plain Epic (score 22) and `2.06` in a full kit (score 27.6: three set pieces at 26 and a Boss Weapon at 30), plus up to `+14%` bonus crit chance and `+48%` health. Set pieces sit 12 above Rare and boss weapons 16 (17 Oct, the owner's call: forty-odd runs for a set has to feel like it); before that the ladder ran 10 / 14 / 18 and a full kit was `1.96`, its boss weapon no stronger than its set pieces. **Every Epic in a season is the same Power** (the owner's call, 18 Oct): Thimblebriar's set pieces are 26 and its weapons 30, like the Barrow's (they were 34 and 38), so a later dungeon's gear is a different way to play, never a bigger number, and the Barrow's is never thrown away. After the first Epic set the climb is your level (XP, Levels & Talents).

**Power, on screen** (17 Oct): every screen calls a hero's or a piece's number **Power**, never item level or ilvl, and shows it as a whole number: Power 10 in Common, 14 in Rare, 22 in plain Epic, 26 for a set piece and 30 for a boss weapon, in any dungeon of the season. It is the weighted score (`score`, `itemScore`), rounded (`wholeIlvl` in `src/meta/gear.ts`; the UI's `ilvl()` in `hearth.ts`). The code and the save keep their own names (`score`, `ilvl`, `recIlvl`, `dropsIlvl`), and the damage multiplier the engine uses (`power`, `1.00` to `2.06` above) comes from it, times your level's bonus in a run.

### The ladder

- 4 slots: Weapon, Helm, Shoulders, Chest.
- Rarity climbs Common → Uncommon → Rare (8 clears) → Epic (from dungeon 3-piece sets and Boss Weapons). Heroes start in Common rags with bronze or wood weapons; item icons go `_0` (Common), `_1`, `_2` to `_3` (Epic), and a hero is drawn in `_rags`, `_iron` or full armour.
- The weapon carries 40% of your power (`WEIGHT.weapon = 4` of `10`), so a Boss Weapon (Power 30 in the Barrow, 4 above its set pieces: `BOSS_WEAPON_EDGE`) is the biggest single jump, about `+38%` damage or healing and `+19%` health over a Rare weapon.
- Every clear drops a regular item better than the best you have in your weakest slot. No dead drops. It waits until you put it on, and raises its role's shared floor, so a new hero of that role starts there.
- Tap any drop (in the collection log or the Dungeons sheet) to hold it up against what that role wears now: Upgrade, Downgrade, Same power or A trade, then what changes (Power, damage or healing, health, set count) and any effect it adds or takes away.
- The armoury lines up each slot the same way everywhere (`shelfFor` in `src/meta/gear.ts`): what is worn, then what waits to go on, then the rest of what you have, best first, then the uniques you haven't found yet. A slot keeps a quiet "something better" mark once its New glow has gone, while anything you have beats what is worn (`betterFor`).
- **No currency:** a duplicate counts in the collection log ("Seen 3×") and your weapon's trait swaps for free. The save still counts each dungeon's `marks` (1 per clear, 3 per duplicate), never shown or spent.

**Weapon traits** (unlocked at Rare, free to swap, and stay active alongside Unique weapons; one picked on a weapon still in the bag works once that weapon is on, `weaponTrait`)

### How sets work

- Each dungeon drops sets per class/role in the Helm, Shoulders and Chest slots.
- Set pieces are uniques from the drop table below at Epic, Power 26 (`+19.2%` damage/healing and `+9.6%` health per piece), and each piece has its own immediate When-Worn ability perk even at 1 of 3 pieces.
- **1 piece (per-piece perk)**: each Helm, Shoulders and Chest piece immediately buffs one of your core abilities when worn.
- **2 pieces**: a set bonus that leans into your role's signature mechanic.
- **3 pieces (`ready: true`)**: changes how one button works with spectral/skeletal summons (`S.thralls`) or ghost saves. All four Barrow 3-piece bonuses are on: `barrowguard3`, `candlewake3`, `rimebone3`, `shadeveil3`.

## Loot

Loot runs on OSRS rules: every unique has its own drop rate, rare means properly rare, and a 1-in-2,500 pet is the flex: a bonus slot in the log (like the mount and dye), never needed to finish it. Each player rolls their own table on every clear, so nobody fights over drops.

**As built:** loot rolls the moment a boss dies (`resolveKill`): every line of its table rolls on its own, per player, and kill counts are per boss. A Heroic kill rolls its normal dungeon's table first, on that boss's normal kill count (what drops is the normal piece, for the normal log), then its own table, the gilding kits and gilded twins, on its own kill count (`kitTables` in `src/content/kits.ts`). A dungeon's seed lines (`seeds` on its `DungeonInfo`, each a `SeedDrop` of `{ boss, seed, rate }`) roll there too. Finishing the run (`resolveClear`) adds the clear: a ladder piece (Common to Rare in eight clears), the chest's XP (`chestXp`: the clear's, its Lore Pages and any tome; "XP, Levels & Talents" above) and a step of growth for every planted bed (`growBeds`). Kills paid their XP during the run. A run that doesn't count (the Crypt Trial, or one started for testing past the entrance or in test gear) gives nothing. The save still counts each dungeon's `marks` (1 a clear, 3 a duplicate; a heroic clear adds to its normal dungeon's), but nothing shows or spends them.

## Heroic

Every dungeon has a harder version, its Heroic, for after the gear cap. Heroic = Normal loot + gilding kits. It never gives more power, and it never gates the next dungeon.

- **Opens** once you've found 3 pieces of the dungeon's Normal collection log (`HEROIC_PIECES`; a duplicate counts once, and the pet, mount and dye count too), never by clears: about 26 runs in one seat on average at the Barrow's rates, more on a dry streak. The owner's call (17 Oct): a drop gate gives the "2/3, one more run" pull a fixed count of clears doesn't. The Dungeons sheet switches between Normal and Heroic on the door; until it opens, the toggle shows "Heroic 1/3", the lock says "Heroic opens once you've found 3 pieces of the Barrow's log. You have 1.", and All dungeons shows "Heroic 1/3 log pieces".
- **Built as** its own `DungeonInfo` (`heroicOf` the normal one) that opens at `HEROIC_PIECES` (3) pieces of the normal one's log (`heroicProgress` in `src/meta/loot.ts`; the testing switch opens it early): the same bosses, and its own tables of kits and gilded twins, built from the normal dungeon's by `kitTables` and `kitLog` in `src/content/kits.ts` (the template's `makeHeroicDungeon` uses the same, so every new dungeon gets this shape).
- **Players:** Normal can always be played solo with your Warband heroes, or with a group. Heroic needs at least two real players; with two, a bot takes the third seat, and your Warband heroes never sit in (`HEROIC_MIN_PLAYERS`, `partyProblem` and `seatsFor` in `src/meta/party.ts`); the players come through Group Finder or friends once online play is live. There is no online play yet, so Heroic stays locked (the "Everything open" testing switch lets you in): on the Dungeons sheet the other two spots at the door are dashed and marked Friend, one line says "Heroic needs 2 players. A bot fills the last seat.", and the gold button stays locked.
- **Harder:** the same dungeon and mechanics, plus the Crypt Seals and the Gravekeeper's full three-phase fight (Normal has two), made for level 20 in full Epic (`hit: 5.0, trashHp: 3.47, bossHp: 2.43`: the full-Epic tune times level 20's bonus, 18 Oct). Bots there clear it in about four minutes without wiping; in Common at level 1 they get buried. Heroic Thimblebriar is made for level 35 in its own kit (`hit: 6.9, trashHp: 9.75, bossHp: 3.9`).
- **Normal loot:** a Heroic kill rolls the normal dungeon's tables line for line, at the same rates and role locks. Those drops are the normal pieces: they fill the Normal log and go on your heroes like any Normal drop, so grouping up is never a worse evening than playing solo. The kill counts on the normal boss's kill count too (its table rolled); Heroic keeps its own kill count, clears and best time.
- **Gilding kits:** then the boss that drops a unique rolls its kit, one per set piece and boss weapon, for any seat at 1/20 (`KIT_RATE`). A kit isn't gear and never goes in a bag: when it drops, that piece's gilded look is yours for good, for every hero (`KITS`, its id the piece's with `.gilded` on the end).
- **The gilded look:** a new kit starts gilded, like every look. On the item sheet and on its card in the armoury, a piece you own whose kit you have switches between Plain and Gilded (`setGilded`; plain is the kit's id in `looksOff`). Same power either way. Gold shows on the gear doll and the armoury (`lookId`), the chest, the log's stands and close-ups, and in the dungeon: wear a gilded piece and your hero shines gold (the Ghostly Dye wins if it's on).
- **Heroic's log:** the kits. The gilded pet, mount and plume are bonus slots. Finishing it gives Gilded Barrow Hall, the Undying and Gilded Bones.
- **The Gravegold Dye** is retired: kits gild the set now. It no longer drops; whoever has one keeps it, and it still turns a Barrow set gold in the dungeon.
- **Achievements:** a heroic clear counts for the dungeon's own achievements.

## The collection log

The log is the trophy room of the chase: OSRS's log with WoW's wardrobe. Every slot shows its rarity (its colour and its word: Epic, Unique, Bonus, Trophy), its drop rate always, and once it's yours the kill count you got it at and how often you've seen it ("Seen 3×"). A slot you haven't got is faded or a dashed ghost, never blacked out: you always see what you're after.

- **A page per dungeon's log.** The log opens straight on a dungeon's page (the first whose log isn't finished); chips switch to the garden's harvests and the other rewards. On the page:
  - **The prize stage:** the log's big prize on a plinth (the Risen, dimmed until earned, with how many slots are left on its plaque), the count in big numbers, a bar with a notch per slot, what finishing gives, the kill counts and the best clear. A Normal / Heroic toggle across the top of the stage (its thumb slides to your side, gold for Heroic) turns the page into the heroic log, in gold with gold outlines, and says what Heroic is: the Normal loot plus gilding kits, each turning one piece gold for good, at the same power. Its stands hold the kits (the pieces gilded), and its shelf the gilded plume, mount and pet, all bonus slots. A finished log lights the prize up and offers its way in: Make a Risen, Put up the look.
  - **The wardrobe:** each set on its own armour stand, its role's boss weapon leaning on it, and its class's badge in the corner (ringed in the role's colour). The pieces you've found are on the stand; the rest are dashed outlines. Under each stand, a tile per piece with a tick or its drop rate; a New ribbon when something arrived, a gold rim when the set is done. A stand opens its set's page; a tile opens a closer look.
  - **The trophy shelf:** the dungeon's trophy and its bonus pet, mount and dye, as their own pictures on a plank, with their rates or kill counts.
- **A set's page:** its stand big with its badge; Try it on shows the whole set in colour (with a Preview stamp); its 2- and 3-piece bonuses, lit when you have the pieces; and a row for every piece with its rarity, slot, rate and effect, and the kill count and date you got it.
- **A closer look:** tapping any slot (a tile under a stand, the shelf, a set's row, the garden's and other pages) opens a card at the bottom of the sheet, over the page and never the whole screen: the thing's own picture big in its rarity's light (a piece you haven't got in full colour, with Not yet), its rarity word, what it is, and when you got it or where it drops (its rate, the boss, your kill count). Gear then has its stats (Power, damage or healing, health), each with how it changes what its role wears now under the verdict (Upgrade, A trade, Wearing it), what it does when worn, and its set's two bonuses lit by how many you have. A gilding kit instead says what it gilds, never power, and whether your piece is gilded. Buttons: Put it on (a piece you own and aren't wearing), The set, and Details (the compare sheet, where a piece with its kit switches between plain and gilded). Tap outside, the close button or Escape closes it, and you're back where you were.
- **In the chest:** a piece the log didn't have gets a moment after its card turns: its stand comes up and the piece drops on, with its rarity, rate and kill count, the set's count (or "set done") and the log's strip ticking up. Tap to carry on; Skip skips it; duplicates don't get it, and a pet or a mount keeps its own big moment. The summary's log strip shows the count going up and opens the log on that set.
- **The art:** the stands are drawn in `tools/art_wardrobe.py` (`art/wardrobe.json`): one bare stand, and every set piece and boss weapon as its own layer with a silhouette and details, and its box for showing it on its own. A missing piece is one dashed line round its whole silhouette (the silhouette masks out its own line, so overlapping shapes don't show) with a faint fill. Each set is drawn from its written `look`, and the set icons are cut from the same pieces, so a tile and its stand agree. A gilding kit is drawn as its piece through `gild()`.
- **As built:** the page is `src/ui/logbook.ts` (the Logs sheet in `logsheet.ts` hands it the collection tab and has its buttons, `logActions`); the closer look is `src/ui/logpeek.ts`; which pieces go on which stand and what goes on the shelf are `standsOf` and `shelfOf` in `src/meta/logs.ts` (a test checks every slot of a log shows exactly once), and `markSeen` there takes the New flags off once the log has shown them; a stand is drawn by `standSvg` and a piece on its own by `pieceSvg`, both in `src/ui/art.ts`; the chest's moment and strip are in `src/ui/reveal.ts`. Nothing new in the save: the log reads `p.collection` (count, kill count and date per find) and `p.seen`.

  - **What you got, and where it went:** `rewardsOf` names each reward with where it went: a title any of your heroes can wear, a trophy or a banner on its spot in the house (which spot), a pet at home, a look on or off in a hero's Looks, a house look in Decorate, a new kind of hero in the bunk room, and the points last.
  - **The toast** says the name, what you did and what you got, and stays about five seconds. Tapping it opens the achievement's card over whatever is showing. Mid-fight the tap goes to the game instead, so a stray tap never opens a panel; the chest lists them all a moment later.
  - **The chest** lists every achievement the run earned: what you did, what you got and where it went, Wear it for a title (on the hero you play), and a tap for its card.
  - **The card** (over the Logs sheet, like the collection log's closer look, ringed in its tier's colour): tier, points and place; what to do (or what you did); your progress, or your best clear against a sprint's mark (`clearUnder`, `bestClear`); the day you earned it and the kill count it came at; and everything it gives, each with where it went and its button: Wear it, See it, Put it up, Make one. The toast, a row, the garden's and the cauldron's chips, and a reward's own sheet and closer look all open it.
  - **The page** (Logs → Achievements): one place at a time (Barrow, Thimblebriar, the garden; a Heroic's go with its dungeon), with chips to move between them and the points, the count and the titles (a tap to wear one) at the top. Each row: what to do, your progress with numbers (370 / 500 so far, 4 / 12, 0 / 17 slots; a sprint shows your best clear), what it gives and where, and once earned the day and the kill count. A hidden one stays ??? until it's yours, then explains itself.
  - **Titles:** every title comes from exactly one achievement (`titleSource`; `checkContent()` checks), which says where it came from and how to earn it. The Armoury's Looks tab lists every title (`titleRows`): yours first, each with where it came from and Wear it (Take it off on the one worn); the rest with exactly how to earn them and your progress; a hidden achievement's stays ???; a retired one's shows only for whoever has it. The title worn shows on your hero's name tag over their head in the house, in the armoury and on their sheet (`wearTitle`: only a title you've earned).
  - **The kill count it came at:** from save v10 an achievement keeps its dungeon's last boss's kill count when it's earned (`kc`); none for the garden's, or before your first kill. Older ones keep their day.
  - **Brewing** counts for the garden's achievements at the cauldron (`homeAchiever`), so Something Brewing pops when you brew your tenth, not at your next harvest.
- **Not right yet:** Bravely Stupid can't be earned: it needs going in alone as the DPS, and only the sims can do that (`solo`); the game always fills the other seats.

## Sharing

Word of mouth is how the game grows, so a moment worth showing off gets a Share button that makes a picture to post in a group chat or a Discord. Built 16 Oct.

- **What's worth showing off** (`bragsOf` in `src/meta/brag.ts`, read from a run's loot and the achievements it earned; nothing is saved): the pet; a whole collection log; a finished set (all three pieces, the last one this run; on Heroic, a gilded set in kits); a new unique (a set piece, a boss weapon, a gilding kit, a mount, the trophy or the dye; a pet or a mount counts even as a duplicate, as the chest gives each its moment); a new best time; an achievement. One run can have several: the best comes first in that order (the rarest unique first), and the card flips to the others.

The pet's and the mount's moment, a new log piece's moment (the last piece of a set shares the set), the filled log's moment and each achievement in the chest have their own. The garden's and the cauldron's achievement chips share too (`achBrag`).

The night sky over the dungeon's floor with its door behind in the haze (from its picture on the Dungeons sheet; for a best time the boss you beat stands in the doorway), the moment's light, and your hero in their gear in their race's light beside what you got: the piece floating, the set on its armour stand, the pet at your feet, you riding the mount, the log's prize hero, the achievement's medal. ## Sound and music

Short, dry and punchy, a TV cartoon's fantasy rather than stock fantasy, so every sound sits well on a phone's speaker.

- **Effects:** 78 real effects made with ElevenLabs: every combat sound, the walkout, the chest and the cards, a log slot filling, putting gear on and the buttons. They are embedded in the page (`audio/sfx.json`, about 320 KB, 40 kbps mono MP3) and each keeps its old synthesised sound (`SOUNDS` in `src/ui/sound.ts`) as its fallback, so a sound that can't be decoded still plays. The garden and brewing sounds, and the gilding kit's little bell (`gild`), are still synthesised only.
- **Your own crits** get their own sound (`crit`), the way only your own numbers show.
- **Hits:** each class's filler has its own impact (`axehit`, `bladehit`, `icehit`, `holyhit`), each payoff its own sound (`crush`, `bless`, `shatter`, `finish`), and there's a heal crit's chime (`healcrit`), a soft tick for swings and ticks (`tap`, at most one every 0.1 s), the boss's Exposed call (`exposed`) and the pack-down chord (`packdown`). Until they get ElevenLabs takes of their own they play as layers of the recorded clips, pitched (`LAYERS` in `src/ui/sound.ts`), each with a synthesised fallback; their entries wait in `tools/audio_list.py`.
- **Music:** three loops made with Eleven Music: the house (a cosy tavern, 46 s), the dungeon (low and tense, 42 s) and a boss (driving, 26 s). They are too big to embed (about 1 MB together), so each one streams from the live page's own files (`music/*.mp3`, published next to the page from `audio/music`) the first time it's wanted, and loops without a gap (`src/ui/music.ts`; `runMusic` picks the loop from a run's state). The boss's loop loads during the dungeon. The reveal is quiet, so every drop's sound lands.
- **The victory sting** (`fanfare`) plays as the last boss falls, at the music's volume (with the music off, the victory jingle plays instead). Under the lantern's tells the music ducks, and for the pet it cuts out with every other sound.
- **Settings:** Sound on or off (off silences the music too), and a Music slider kept on this device (`highfell.music` in local storage), never in the save.
- **Making sounds:** `tools/audio.py` asks ElevenLabs for takes, trims each to its first sound and natural end, takes out rumble, levels its loudness against the others and compresses it; `tools/audio_list.py` holds every prompt and which take went in.

## Onboarding

A fresh save opens on the hero maker.

- **The first hero** gets the Crypt Trial's chest at once (`grantTrialStarter`, once a save): an Uncommon weapon and helm waiting in its bag (until 17 Oct the helm went to another role's shared floor), so skipping or quitting the trial loses nothing.
- **The Crypt Trial** (`start: 'intro'`): 60 seconds as that hero in full Epic test gear, in the Ossuary and Sir Rattlebones's tomb. Brannoc tanks unless you are the tank, and your seat gets its role's Barrow weapon and 2-piece bonus. A Welcome card offers the trial or a skip.
- **Five teaching moments** drawn for your class (`TUT_BY_CLASS` in `src/ui/run.ts`): your opener, your pack or peel button, `Dodge` out of `Shield Charge`'s red lane, the heavy swing (a tank perfect-blocks it; a cleric Wards, a mage throws Icicle, a rogue Shadowsteps, and he staggers) and your big hit in the `Exposed` window. Each one pauses the game, spotlights the button to press (TAP! on it, or CLICK! with a mouse; the other buttons dim and go quiet) and waits for that button, or its key on a keyboard. The card has no button of its own, and a touch, swipe or click on the dungeon does nothing while it waits, so the button you learn is the one you'll press in a run (17 Oct, after a play test where a tap on the screen, not the Roll button, finished the dodge lesson; a wrong button shakes). Heroes cannot drop below 20% health, and `Skip intro` is always there.
- **The chest:** killing Sir Rattlebones plays the gold `unique` walkout and opens the trial's chest (`reveal.showTrial`): the two starter pieces, plus a 1/25 set piece and a 1/50 trophy stamped `Example` (that is what the gold lantern was about), with the Barrow's collection log and its three prizes.
- **The Hearth tour:** then a guided tour of the Hearth (`hall` your hero's gear → `bunks` the empty bed → `cellar` the Barrow's door; until your Warband opens it skips the bunk room, two steps instead of three), which ends on the whole house (finished or skipped). The trial and the tour both replay from Settings; a replayed trial's chest is all examples.
- **Your Warband, at the door:** the first time you open the Dungeons once your Warband is open (on Normal; Heroic seats friends, never your heroes), a Your Warband tip under the door: your other heroes fill the other seats in their own gear, better gear on them makes runs faster, and a bot stands in until you make one. It stays until the last bot seat gets a hero or you tap Later; tapping a seat or going in also counts as seen.
- **The garden's tips:** fresh saves start with no seed, and a save's very first run never drops one. Then a one-step tip as each first comes, pinned on what to tap (the pin does it too): your first seed (the yard's beds: plant it), your first ready bed (pick it), your first herbs that make a brew (the cauldron) and your first bottle (Your Brew, by the brew button on the door: one bottle is poured as you go in, for the whole party). A tip waits while something is open over the house (a harvest's card, a sheet) and shows once it closes. A save that has already planted, picked or brewed never gets that one; the door's two tips show once to every save.
- **Your first talent points** (17 Oct): once your level gives points and no hero of yours has put one in, a one-step tip pins your hero by the fire (Tap Your Hero · Talents) after the welcome tour; the pin opens the armoury's Talents tab. Your hero's tag at the top of the house says your level and, when the gear has nothing new, a gold badge counts the points to spend.
- **As built:** which tip is due is `houseTip` and `doorTip` in `src/meta/tips.ts`, and `finishTip` marks one seen; the house's steps are data in `src/ui/tours.ts` (the ready tip pins the bed that is ready). It all lives in `p.counters` (`intro.tour`, `intro.seed`, `tip.talents`, `tip.ready`, `tip.herbs`, `tip.warband`, `tip.brew`), so the save's shape never changes.
- **Learning a class, in the moment:** a class's first five runs glow the one button whose moment has come (`momentFor` in `src/dungeon/coach.js`), with a short word on it. A roll out of a hit about to land comes first, then each class's own reads, most urgent first. The healer Wards before a big hit lands on the tank, Sunbursts when two mates are under half or anyone is cursed ("Lift!"), taps a hurt mate's frame ("Tap", the frame glows instead of a button), drops a Prayer circle when the party is bunched and Smites when everyone is topped up. ## Content Scope, Release Tiers & Stepped Unique Treadmill

### Making a new dungeon

A new dungeon starts as a copy of the template, `src/content/_template/`: a small working dungeon (bots clear
it in about 3:15 in Common gear) that isn't in the game. Its `README.md` is the guide. The rules for every
new dungeon:

- **One signature idea:** the one mechanic the dungeon is about, used by its trash and both its bosses (the
  `signature` slot in its engine def). The template's example is beacons, which pulse the party until a DPS
  breaks them and shield the boss who planted them.
- **One or two new enemy types;** the rest are the shared kinds (melee, ranged, brute, caller, mender) in the
  dungeon's dress. Every mob has one job (fodder, a shooter, a brute, a caster or mender with a cast to kick, a
  curser, the signature), and each pack is fodder plus one or two of the others.
- **Made for a level:** the first dungeon of a season is made for Common at level 1; a later one recommends a level and a Power (`recLevel`, `recIlvl`) and is tuned so bots at that level in plain gear of that Power clear it in 3 to 4 minutes. Its gear is the season's Power (26 a set piece, 30 a boss weapon), never more: its sets and weapons are different, and levels do the climbing.
- **Two bosses of two or three moves:** the mini boss about 40 seconds, the final boss two phases and about 80
  seconds, every move built from the shared parts (`src/dungeon/moves.js`: cone, line and circle telegraphs,
  Exposed windows, adds, a party pulse, a cast), and one clear job for each role in each fight. Every boss has a
  tank buster to block and a window to burn, and a hit on the whole party is a cast to kick (`cast()`).
- **A different layout shape:** layouts are data (`src/dungeon/layout.js`): any number of rooms, doors on any
  side, branches, side rooms, round rooms. Barrow Crypt is the line of rooms; the next ones are not.
- **No puzzle on Normal:** the three-seat seal is a Heroic-only hook.
- **The art is the main cost:** the 3-piece sets, the boss weapons and their armour stands in the collection
  log, then the bosses and the new enemies. The rest is reused.
- **Its door and its Heroic come with the template:** `stage.ts` is its door on the Dungeons sheet, and
  `heroic.ts` builds its Heroic (`makeHeroicDungeon`: the Normal loot plus gilding kits).

`tests/template.test.ts` keeps the template to these rules, and `npm run sim -- --dungeon <id>` times any
dungeon's packs and bosses.

### Seasons

The owner's direction is seasons, not expansions (`docs/ideas.md`). Built so far (17 Oct): each dungeon says which season it came in (`season` on its `DungeonInfo`, a number; the name the game shows is in `SEASONS` in `src/content/index.ts`, and a Heroic is in its dungeon's season). Barrow Crypt, Thimblebriar Hollow and Moonmere Sanctum are **Season 0** (Moonmere, not built yet, has it on its card in the Dungeons sheet's list). The Dungeons sheet groups All dungeons under each season's name and tags the door. Every Epic in a season is the same Power (18 Oct), and when a season comes, levels reset with the gear (the owner's call, 18 Oct). Nothing else about seasons is built: no new season, no timewalking, no reset. The tiers and the Act 1 trio below are the older plan (their stepped Power is gone: every dungeon of a season drops Power 26 and 30), kept until the season direction gets its own pull request.

### Zero Currency Bloat: Killing Marks, Free Weapon Traits & Dungeon Seeds
- **No Marks / No Currency Shops:** All cosmetics, mounts, pets, house looks, trophies, and gear come exclusively from dungeon/raid drops, collection logs, and achievements.
- **Weapon Traits Toggle for Free at Rare+ (`ilvl 18`):** Once your regular weapon reaches Rare (~Clear 8), all 3 Weapon Traits unlock for that hero and can be swapped freely in the Gear screen.
- **Dungeon Seeds Feed the Garden:** Each dungeon's final boss drops its own seed at a fixed rate (the Barrow's Gravekeeper: Glowcap, `1/5` Normal, `1/3` Heroic). Seeds grow in the Hearth yard's 3 planter beds over 3 clears and harvest into herbs for the cauldron (see The Garden & Brews).
- **Duplicate Uniques (`p.collection[id].n`) Saved for Late-Game Synthesis:** Duplicates stay tracked on every item in the Collection Log (`Seen 3 times`) for late-game synthesis recipes (combining duplicate weapons + boss-dropped Awakening Orbs from later dungeons/raids).
